School calendar

Upload your school's academic calendar once. The app reads it, you check the dates, and from then on it knows your semesters, weeks, days off, study break and exams. It skips classes on days there are none and numbers your weeks. Example: Vanier College 2026–27.

1 · Upload it in Settings

A new group in Settings. Before a calendar is loaded, there's one button. Afterwards, it names the school, says where you are in the year, and offers to review, replace or remove it.

2 · Check what it found

The AI reads the PDF, then you see everything before it's saved: the semesters (first day, last day of classes, end of exams) and each special day with its kind. Fix a date, change a kind, delete a row, or add one by hand. Only the kinds change how the app behaves; the labels are just names.

No classes: holidays and closures. Class meetings are hidden that day; the cell is hatched.

Study break: no classes, green, and the week isn't counted in "Week N of M".

Exams: the exam period. Regular classes are hidden; your exam items still show.

Follows Monday: that date runs another weekday's timetable. You see Monday's classes instead of its own.

Marker: a date worth seeing that changes nothing: mid-point, course drop deadline, withdrawal deadline.

Outside every semester (summer, the winter gap) is shown as "No school" and has no classes.

Things that don't matter to your timetable, like registration periods and grade submission deadlines, are left out by the AI. You can add them as markers.

3 · The month view

October 2026. The election on the 5th and Thanksgiving on the 12th are hatched and have no classes. On Tuesday the 6th you get Monday's classes. The mid-point and the mid-term deadline are small markers. The header says which week of the semester the month starts in.

4 · The week board

The week's title gets its number in the semester. A study break or exam week says so instead, and days off are hatched with their reason.
