Study Buddies

A pixel creature for each Rounds theme. It hatches from an egg and evolves through seven ranks as you study, picking up its theme's gear as it grows. Its mood shows your health: happy when you keep up, tired, then sick, then bandaged when things slip. It never dies, and XP and coins are never lost. Or make your own: upload any picture and it becomes a custom buddy.
