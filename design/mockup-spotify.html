The Spotify strip on the coat

A quiet strip at the bottom of the sidebar, under the pocket, showing what is playing on the signed-in user's Spotify with previous, play/pause and next. It sits inside the coat, in the Ward Board palette, and the only green on the page is Spotify's own mark. Built to the approved spec; this page uses the app's real stylesheets, so what you see is what ships.

In place

Desktop, something playing. The pocket keeps its spot; the strip hangs beneath it, separated by a hairline tinted with the coat colour. Album art is 36px, the title links to the track on Spotify, the artist line truncates, and the thin progress line moves once a second between polls.
