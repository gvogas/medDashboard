Four ways to wear the player

The first mockup is a quiet strip under the pocket. Here it is beside three other directions: a badge clipped to the coat, an album-art card, and a pill in the top bar that opens a small panel. All four use the same routes and poller from the spec; only the front end changes.

Recommendation

B, the badge. It keeps the spec's placement and API, costs 30px more than the strip, and fixes the strip's two real weaknesses: it looks like something worn on the coat rather than a footer, and it tells you where Spotify is playing before you press a button. The moving bars replace the need for a pause icon to tell playing from paused. If you want it quieter, drop the bars and keep the device line. C is the prettiest and the one to revisit if the sidebar ever grows wider; D is the fallback if phone visibility matters more than the coat.
