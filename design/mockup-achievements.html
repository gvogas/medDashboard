Your own, as stickers

Make your own achievements. Pick a sticker, give it a name, and choose what earns it: hours studied on one course, or a grade on one exam or quiz. The app watches for it and sticks it on, at a slight tilt, when you get there. Until then it stays grey and shows how far along you are.

What counts

Study hours: total minutes across every session, timer or logged by hand. Dated the day the total crossed the line.

Streak: your longest streak ever, using the same rules as the streak tile (daily minimum, rest days). A broken streak never takes a badge away.

Big days: your first session, the first day you hit your daily goal, and a 4-hour day.

Tasks done: tasks, assignments, labs, projects and other items marked Done, Submitted or Graded. Exams and quizzes count separately.

Exams: exams and quizzes marked Taken or Graded.

Grades: an exam or quiz graded 80+, 90+, or 100. One great grade earns all the tiers below it too.
