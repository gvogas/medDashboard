What gets built once you pick

All three directions run on the same structure, so the choice is purely visual — nothing below changes.

  • Six linked databases — assignments, exams, labs, tutoring, office hours, loose tasks — each with course, date/time, location, status and notes.
  • Week board, month calendar and a filterable table view over all of them at once.
  • Notion-style pages under every item for notes, checklists and links.
  • Countdowns and status — days to the next exam, overdue flagged, submitted and graded tracked.
  • Saved in your browser to start; say the word and I'll wire it to real storage so it follows you across devices.

Tell me “build A”, “build B” or “build C” — or mix them: A's layout with B's dark mode is a normal request.
