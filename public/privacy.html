Term privacy policy

Draft, last updated 8 October 2026. Review before publishing.

What Term keeps

  • Your account: your username, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), your display name and your settings.
  • What you add: courses, class times, items (exams, assignments and the rest), grades, notes, study sessions, achievements and quotes.
  • Shared classes: what you add to a shared class is visible to its members; your own status, grades and notes on it are not.
  • Term Pro: whether you have Pro and when it renews or ends. Apple takes the payment; Term never sees your card. RevenueCat, which checks purchases for Term, receives your Term user id and your purchase history.
  • Spotify, if you connect it: an encrypted token that lets Term show and control your music. Disconnect it in Settings at any time.
  • Security records: counts of failed sign-ins and requests, kept for a short time to stop abuse, and an error log without your content.

Files you import with AI

A course outline, school calendar or timetable you import is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to be read, and the answer is shown to you before anything is saved. Term does not keep the file.

Where it is kept

Term runs on Cloudflare (Workers and the D1 database). A copy of your data is also kept on each device you use, so Term works offline.

What Term does not do

No ads, no selling or sharing of your data for advertising, no tracking across other apps or sites.

Notifications

Reminders in the iPhone and Mac app are worked out and scheduled on your device. Nothing about them is sent to a server.

Deleting your data

Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account and everything you added, for good. Export your data first if you want a copy. Deleting does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple account settings.
